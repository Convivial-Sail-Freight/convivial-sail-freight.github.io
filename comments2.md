All of this is experimental. We're trying to explore low-energy, low-carbon, high-relationship ways of living. A lot of this is in order to connect people and establish relationships, give kids a sense of adventure. There's also a history lesson aspect where we look back to the history of sail cargo in the area and learn about how that worked. We learn about older forms of the economy and how these techniques from before fossil fuels might inform a lower-energy future.
